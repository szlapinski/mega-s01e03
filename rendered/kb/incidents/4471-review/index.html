Why these are different problems

The shared parser reads month first even when the tenant’s date order is day first. Harbour Dental’s app report R-41200 describes 7 April turning into 4 July. Morrow Tax’s app report R-41205 belongs to parser even though intake tagged it hint. A parser fix needs tenant-aware or unambiguous date handling across app, email and booking-v2 inputs.

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY but reads input in the day-first tenant’s order. Copper Studio’s form report R-41230 belongs to hint even though intake tagged it parser. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour use this form. Fixing shared parsing alone does not repair a misleading hint. The desk instead retired the affected form.

One-day and scheduling symptoms remain flagged within the classifier’s parser group. The engineering closure does not independently demonstrate that these particular symptoms were caused by parsing or that the fix resolved them.
